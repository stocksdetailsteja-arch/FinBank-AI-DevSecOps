# 🚨 Advanced Configuration Drift RCA Playbook

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Every scenario follows Symptoms, Evidence, Investigation, Root Cause, Fix, Rollback, Prevention, and Banking Impact.


## Scenario 1: Auto-Remediation Restarts Payment Service

**Symptoms:** Configuration, service, or governance evidence indicates a deviation or failed convergence.
**Evidence:** Desired state, actual state, diff, checksums, Git history, change records, logs, SLOs, and transaction signals.
**Investigation:** Validate ownership, approval, dependencies, environment scope, reload behavior, and banking impact.
**Root Cause:** Record the proven technical or process cause.
**Fix:** Apply an authorized and tested correction.
**Rollback:** Restore the known-good configuration, image, traffic path, or forward fix.
**Prevention:** Improve policy, CI, secrets handling, canary scope, monitoring, expiry, and audit evidence.
**Banking Impact:** Validate authentication, payments, idempotency, queues, databases, ledger, fraud controls, and audit continuity.
