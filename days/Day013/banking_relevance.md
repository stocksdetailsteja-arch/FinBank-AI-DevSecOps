# 🏦 Banking Audit & Incident Readiness

Payment, fraud, authentication, ledger and reconciliation systems require attributable access, synchronized timestamps, protected evidence and business-state validation. Security containment must account for transaction uncertainty. A process or service recovery is incomplete until queued, posted and uncertain transactions are reconciled.

### Scenario
A privileged login and payment-service alert occur in the same window. Preserve evidence, validate whether events are related, restrict unnecessary access through approved controls, and reconcile payment outcomes before closure.
