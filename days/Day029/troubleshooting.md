# 🚨 Advanced Payment Intent RCA Playbook

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Every scenario follows Symptoms, Evidence, Investigation, Root Cause, Fix, Rollback, Prevention and Banking Impact.


## Scenario 1: Duplicate Intent Created

**Symptoms:** Intent identity, state, account validation, amount or API result is incorrect.
**Evidence:** Request, status, response, in-memory store, account records, source revision and logs.
**Investigation:** Isolate parsing, validation, sequence generation, state assignment or serialization.
**Root Cause:** Record the proven defect.
**Fix:** Correct the responsible layer and rerun intent plus full regression tests.
**Rollback:** Restore the last known-good revision and restart through the root lifecycle.
**Prevention:** Add idempotency, durable uniqueness, concurrency control, authorization, audit and state invariants.
**Banking Impact:** Duplicate or invalid intents can cause repeated payment attempts, customer harm and control failure.
