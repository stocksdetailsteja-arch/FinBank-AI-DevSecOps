# 🏦 Banking Observability, Auditability & Transaction Integrity

Observability must answer: what happened, when, where, which transaction, what state was authoritative, what customer impact occurred, and how recovery was proven.

## Business Validation
- payment status and idempotency
- gateway and database evidence
- queue and dead-letter state
- ledger posting and balances
- fraud/authentication dependency health
- audit-event continuity
- customer-impact and regulatory assessment

> [!IMPORTANT]
> A log line saying `success` is not authoritative proof of financial completion. Reconcile against the system of record.
