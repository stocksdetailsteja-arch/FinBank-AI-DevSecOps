# 🏦 Banking Host Hardening

- **Confidentiality:** minimize access to customer and transaction data.
- **Integrity:** protect binaries, configurations, ledgers and audit records.
- **Availability:** harden without breaking payment, fraud, authentication or reconciliation services.
- **Segregation:** separate operations, development, database, security and audit privilege.
- **Evidence:** retain attributable security and change records.
- **Recovery:** preserve break-glass and rollback paths for SSH, firewall and MAC changes.

### Scenario
A payment service needs a new outbound endpoint after firewall tightening. Validate ownership and business need, test narrow egress, monitor transactions, and retain an approved exception with expiry.
