# 🚨 Advanced Ansible Production RCA Playbook

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Every scenario follows Symptoms, Evidence, Investigation, Root Cause, Fix, Rollback, Prevention, and Banking Impact.


## Scenario 1: Playbook Targets Entire Production Group

**Symptoms:** Automation, service, or governance evidence indicates unsafe scope, repeated change, failed convergence, or business regression.
**Evidence:** Inventory graph, resolved variables, task output, recap, logs, controller identity, approvals, SLOs, and transaction signals.
**Investigation:** Validate host pattern, precedence, module semantics, handler notification, privilege, artifact version, and business impact.
**Root Cause:** Record the proven automation, content, inventory, credential, or governance cause.
**Fix:** Apply an authorized and tested correction with bounded scope.
**Rollback:** Restore the known-good artifact or configuration, revert traffic, recover data, or forward-fix.
**Prevention:** Add CI policy, linting, canary, serial batches, secrets controls, monitoring, and approval gates.
**Banking Impact:** Validate authentication, payments, idempotency, queues, databases, ledger, fraud controls, and audit continuity.
