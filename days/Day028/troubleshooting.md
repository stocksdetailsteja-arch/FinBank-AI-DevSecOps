# 🚨 Advanced Payment Service RCA Playbook

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Every scenario follows Symptoms, Evidence, Investigation, Root Cause, Fix, Rollback, Prevention and Banking Impact.


## Scenario 1: Valid Quote Returns 400

**Symptoms:** Quote status, amount, fee, currency or regression behavior is incorrect.
**Evidence:** Query input, HTTP status, response JSON, service result, policy version, build revision and logs.
**Investigation:** Reproduce the exact amount and isolate parsing, validation, fee calculation, serialization or lifecycle ambiguity.
**Root Cause:** Record the proven defect.
**Fix:** Correct the responsible layer and rerun payment plus full regression tests.
**Rollback:** Restore the last known-good revision and restart through the root lifecycle.
**Prevention:** Add boundary tests, money invariants, policy versioning, idempotency, authorization separation and audit evidence.
**Banking Impact:** Incorrect payment pricing or lifecycle interpretation can create customer harm, fee disputes, duplicate processing or accounting errors.
