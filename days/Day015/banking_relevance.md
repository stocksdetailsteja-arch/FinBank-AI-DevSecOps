# 🏦 Banking Storage Governance

Banking storage must preserve transaction integrity, audit continuity, predictable latency, encryption, retention, and recoverability.

## Validation Beyond `df`
- payment commit and idempotency state
- ledger balances and sequence
- fraud decision evidence
- authentication/key availability
- queue and dead-letter health
- audit/log persistence

> [!IMPORTANT]
> A healthy filesystem does not prove a healthy banking workload. Technical and financial control totals must both pass.
