# 🔐 Assessment Security, Privacy & Evidence Governance

> [!NOTE]
> Day020 uses read-only aggregate metadata and synthetic findings. It does not install a scanner, run intrusive probes, publish real vulnerabilities, modify controls, or claim compliance certification.

## Why This Matters

Vulnerability discovery is only the start. Enterprise governance validates applicability, combines technical and banking context, assigns ownership, authorizes treatment, proves service safety, rescans, and retains closure evidence.

## Production and Banking Lens

Every decision protects payment availability, authentication, fraud controls, queues, databases, ledgers, reconciliation, audit continuity, and recovery objectives.

| Risk | Prevent | Detect | Respond |
|---|---|---|---|
| credential compromise | least privilege and secure storage | usage monitoring | revoke and rotate |
| scanner overload | tested concurrency and windows | SLO monitoring | pause or throttle |
| finding disclosure | classification and restricted access | leak scanning | contain and investigate |
| incomplete coverage | ownership and credential health | coverage dashboard | rerun and document gap |
| stale exception | owner and expiry | governance reporting | escalate and remediate |

## Guardrails

Never commit real findings, private endpoints, credentials, scanner tokens, internal URLs, or full production inventories. Preserve authoritative evidence in the approved system of record.
