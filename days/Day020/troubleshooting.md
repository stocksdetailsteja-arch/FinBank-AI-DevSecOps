# 🚨 Advanced Vulnerability & Compliance RCA Playbook

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Every scenario follows Symptoms, Evidence, Investigation, Root Cause, Fix, Rollback, Prevention, and Banking Impact.


## Scenario 1: False Positive from Backported Fix

**Symptoms:** Assessment, service, or governance evidence indicates a possible failure.
**Evidence:** Source output, vendor evidence, host metrics, transaction signals, ownership, and approvals.
**Investigation:** Validate scope, applicability, exposure, control operation, and business impact.
**Root Cause:** Record the proven technical or governance cause rather than an assumption.
**Fix:** Apply the authorized treatment and verify the control.
**Rollback:** Restore the tested safe state or use an approved forward fix.
**Prevention:** Improve scope, scanner tuning, ownership, monitoring, expiry, and regression tests.
**Banking Impact:** Validate payments, authentication, queues, ledger, reconciliation, fraud controls, and audit continuity.
