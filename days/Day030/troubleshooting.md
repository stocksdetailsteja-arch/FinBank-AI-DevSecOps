# 🚨 Advanced Authorization RCA Playbook

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Every scenario follows Symptoms, Evidence, Investigation, Root Cause, Fix, Rollback, Prevention and Banking Impact.


## Scenario 1: Missing Intent Authorized

**Symptoms:** Authorization status, state, identity, concurrency or control behavior is incorrect.
**Evidence:** Request, response, intent before and after, logs, source revision and test report.
**Investigation:** Isolate route parsing, state lookup, transition validation, replacement, concurrency or control integration.
**Root Cause:** Record the proven defect.
**Fix:** Correct the responsible layer and rerun authorization plus full regression.
**Rollback:** Restore the last known-good revision and restart through the root lifecycle.
**Prevention:** Add persistent versioning, optimistic locking, authenticated actor, idempotency, fraud, sanctions and immutable audit.
**Banking Impact:** Invalid authorization can enable unauthorized payments, duplicates, customer loss and regulatory breach.
