# Banking Relevance

## Controlled change
Banking platforms require clear ownership and traceability. Repository isolation and reviewed commits create a defensible change trail.

## Transaction integrity
Later payment workflows will require idempotency, atomic ledger posting, reconciliation and immutable business events. Day 001 introduces the evidence discipline needed to diagnose them.

## Availability
A baseline allows engineers to compare healthy and degraded states. CPU, memory, disk, network and process information become part of incident evidence.

## Security and privacy
Real customer records and payment data are explicitly excluded from this learning repository. All future examples use synthetic data.

## AI boundary
The Day 001 AI workflow is advisory and read-only. No AI system can approve payments, expose customer data, disable controls or perform privileged remediation.
