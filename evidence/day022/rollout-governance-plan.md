# Day022 Rollout Governance Plan
## Preconditions
Protected source, trusted artifact, approved inventory, bounded credentials, syntax validation, check mode, canary, monitoring and recovery.
## Progressive Rollout
Use explicit limits, tags and serial batches; stop on breach of failure or banking thresholds.
## Idempotency
Repeat execution must report no unnecessary change after convergence.
## Rollback
Restore known-good configuration or artifact, revert traffic, recover data, or forward-fix.
## Banking Validation
Validate authentication, payments, idempotency, queues, database, ledger, fraud controls and audit.
## Closure
Retain source revision, approval, execution recap, validation, exception and recovery evidence.
