# Day020 Remediation Decision
## Decision
Use trusted evidence, applicability validation, effective-risk priority, authorized treatment, rescan and banking validation.
## Treatment
Patch, mitigate, remove, isolate, replace, accept with expiry, or dispute with evidence.
## Rollback
Use tested configuration, application, data, traffic or forward-fix recovery.
## Banking Validation
Validate authentication, payments, idempotency, queues, database, ledger, fraud controls and audit.
